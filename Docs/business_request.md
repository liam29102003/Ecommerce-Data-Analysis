## NovaMart — Business Request

### Background

NovaMart is a growing online retail company selling products across multiple cities. The company has been collecting data on customers, products, orders, payments, and returns.

The management team currently receives basic operational reports, but they do not have a consolidated view of **sales performance, customer behavior, payment activity, and product/return performance**.

The Head of Commercial Operations has asked the Data Analytics team to conduct an analysis of the company's recent sales data and identify areas where the business can improve.

### Business Request

> **"We need a clearer understanding of how our e-commerce business is performing and where we are losing opportunities."**

Management would like an analysis covering:

1. **Overall sales performance** 
   -  How much revenue are we generating? 
   -  How are sales changing over time? 
   -  Which periods have the strongest and weakest performance? 
2. **Product performance** 
   -  Which products and brands generate the most sales? 
   -  Which products have high sales volume but relatively low revenue? 
   -  Are there products that appear to be underperforming? 
3. **Customer performance** 
   -  Which customer segments or locations contribute the most revenue? 
   -  How concentrated is our revenue among customers? 
   -  Are there cities with particularly strong or weak customer activity? 
4. **Payment performance** 
   -  Which payment methods are most commonly used? 
   -  Does payment behavior differ across customers or locations? 
   -  Are there unusual payment patterns that require investigation? 
5. **Returns** 
   -  What percentage of orders are being returned? 
   -  Which products or categories have the highest return activity? 
   -  What are the most common reasons for returns? 
   -  Are certain locations or products associated with unusually high return rates? 
6. **Operational performance** 
   -  Are there inconsistencies between order and payment dates? 
   -  Are there missing or inconsistent values affecting reporting? 
   -  Are there data-quality issues that management should be aware of before making decisions? 

### Expected Outcome

The analysis should produce:

-  A cleaned and validated analytical dataset 
-  SQL analysis answering the key business questions 
-  A set of meaningful KPIs 
-  Identification of important trends and anomalies 
-  A Power BI dashboard for management 
-  Business recommendations based on the findings 

### Management's Main Goal

The ultimate goal is to help NovaMart answer:

> **"What is driving our sales performance, where are we experiencing problems, and what areas should management prioritize for improvement?"**
